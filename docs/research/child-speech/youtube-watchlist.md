# Watch-only list: real 3–7 year old speech

For listening and writing notes by hand only. Do not download videos or captions (YouTube's terms forbid it, and these are third-party and children's content).

## UK
- [1962: What do '60s kids want for Christmas? (BBC Archive)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lhRKO95arSA): grotto wish answers
- [The Secret Life of 5 Year Olds: Resisting Temptations (Channel 4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e2oNXXX8nso): age 5
- [The Secret Life of 4, 5 and 6 Year Olds S04 E01 (Channel 4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFM63Hrviro): ages 4–6
- [The Secret Life of 4 Year Olds: Fighting Talk (Channel 4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R-sv5mtcKMM): age 4

## Listen for
1. Answer length
2. Trailing off, fillers, pauses
3. Topic jumps
4. "I don't know" and changing their answer
5. Echoing the adult or only answering a choice

## Better data source
Record consenting friends' or family kids (with a parent present), asking Aunty Bella's real questions, and transcribe with ElevenLabs Scribe.
